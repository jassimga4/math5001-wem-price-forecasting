## Exact linear identities (do not include both sides in a linear model)

- `sent_out_mw − 2×sent_out_mwh  max |error| = 0`
- `scada_mw − 12×scada_mwh  max |error| = 0`
- `residual_demand_mw − (demand − DPV)  max |error| = 0`
- `is_weekend == (dow ≥ 5)  identity holds: True`

Recommended drops for linear / GLM-style models:

- `sent_out_mwh` (keep `sent_out_mw`)
- `scada_mwh` (keep `scada_mw`)
- `unscheduled_demand_mw` (keep `operational_demand_mw`)
- either `residual_demand_mw` **or** the pair (`operational_demand_mw`, `dpv_mw`), not all three
- `is_weekend` **or** `dow`, not both as numeric
- contemporaneous `rtp` if the task is to forecast MCP (leakage)

## Modelling takeaways

1. **Horizon first.** `mcp_lag1` will crush a 5-minute-ahead point forecast and is illegal for day-ahead. Split feature sets by horizon.
2. **Do not leak RTP.** Same-interval `rtp` is a 30-minute energy price built from the same market as MCP.
3. **Collapse the demand block.** Keep `residual_demand_mw` (or demand + DPV) plus at most one of SCADA / scheduled / sent-out.
4. **STEM is the clean forward price.** `stem_price` is the least-leaky price-level covariate in the panel.
5. **Calendar is nonlinear.** Encode `hour`, `dow`, `month` as cyclic or categorical; `year` is a regime dummy, not a trend to extrapolate.
6. **FCAS is a separate market.** Use lagged FCAS, or model it separately; same-interval FCAS is not an energy fundamental.
7. **Score on ranks and tails.** High kurtosis in MCP means pinball / interval coverage / CRPS should lead; RMSE is spike-dominated.
8. **Fill or drop 30-min generation gaps.** `dpv_mw_30min` (~5% missing) and `sent_out_*` (~3% missing) are the only material holes.
